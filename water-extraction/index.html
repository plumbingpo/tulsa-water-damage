Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Tulsa, Tulsa Restoration Co brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age

When to Call for Water Extraction in Tulsa

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
