A dry house is not yet a finished house. Once the moisture readings are back to normal, there are still cut walls, missing flooring and bare subfloor to deal with. Water damage repair is the rebuild. In Tulsa, we put back the materials that were lost and match them to the originals, turning a work zone into a normal room again.

No two repair jobs are the same size. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. Large jobs can include insulation, whole drywall sheets, subfloor, laminate or hardwood, cabinet boxes, doors and trim. Our aim is a like-for-like replacement, and when a true match cannot be found we say so directly and let you decide.

We do not close anything in while it is still damp. A moisture meter has to show normal levels in the subfloor and framing before new material is installed. Mold hidden in a newly painted wall usually traces back to someone covering damp lumber. You also receive a written scope and schedule ahead of the work, which spells out what gets rebuilt and when.

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's what you can expect when you choose Tulsa Restoration Co:

Why Hire a Professional for Water Damage Repair?

This is not the same as a standard renovation project. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Tulsa

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • A water stain should stay unpainted and unpatched while its cause is still active.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
