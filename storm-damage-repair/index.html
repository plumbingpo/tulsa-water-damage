Heavy rainstorms, hurricanes, tropical storms and flash floods can do serious water damage to homes and buildings in Tulsa. Wind-driven rain pushes water in through damaged roofing, broken windows and loose siding. A roof leak can soak insulation, ceilings and the cavities inside your walls. During a flash flood, drains cannot keep up and water ends up in the lowest floors. When a storm damages your property, Tulsa Restoration Co responds on an emergency basis to limit water intrusion, extract standing water and start restoration right away.

Our storm damage repair covers the whole job, from emergency board-up and tarping that keep more water out, through water extraction, structural drying and rebuilding what was damaged. One problem tends to trigger the next after a storm, as a roof opening admits water, wet ceilings and walls break down, and poor drying leads to mold growth. We address all of these connected issues under one coordinated restoration plan.

After storm damage your insurer will want records, and we supply them: photos, moisture readings, written damage assessments and a detailed scope of work. Our team stays in direct contact with your insurance carrier during the claim and can advocate for a fair review of what the storm did to your property.

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Our technicians trace where the water traveled using thermal imaging plus moisture meters, so no affected area is overlooked.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. An improperly installed tarp can create new problems by channeling water or failing under wind load. We have done a lot of storm mitigation, and we use suitable materials and methods so the temporary protection holds until permanent repairs are on the calendar.

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Tulsa

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
